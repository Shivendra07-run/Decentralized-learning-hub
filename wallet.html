Security First

Wallet Safety & Self-Custody Rules

With great sovereignty comes absolute personal responsibility. Follow these non-negotiable security principles to safeguard your digital property.

Never Share Your Seed Phrase

Your 12 or 24-word recovery phrase is the master key to all your accounts. Anyone who knows it can take all your funds instantly. Write it down on paper and store it offline.

MetaMask Never Asks for It

No genuine customer support agent, developer, admin, or website will ever ask for your secret recovery phrase. Any pop-up or email asking for your seed phrase is an outright phishing scam.

Beware of Fake Sites & DMs

Scammers clone popular DEXs and NFT marketplaces with lookalike domain names (e.g. app.unisvvap.com). Always bookmark official URLs and never click links in direct messages.

Check the URL & Signatures

Carefully inspect the signature prompt in MetaMask before clicking confirm. Beware of setApprovalForAll or permit signatures from unknown contracts, which grant permission to drain tokens.

Use Test Networks to Learn

Practice transactions on testnets like Sepolia or Polygon Amoy first. Testnet coins are free from public faucets and have no financial value, making them the safest training ground.

Hardware Wallets for Value

For meaningful amounts of capital, use a physical hardware wallet (e.g. Ledger, Trezor). A hardware wallet isolates your private keys on a dedicated secure offline chip that never touches your browser.
